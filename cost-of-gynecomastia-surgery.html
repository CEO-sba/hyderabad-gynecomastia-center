What Affects the Cost?

The final cost of gynecomastia surgery depends on several factors. The grade and severity of your condition is the primary factor, as higher grades require more extensive surgical work. The specific technique used (liposuction only vs. combined liposuction and excision) also affects pricing. Anaesthesia requirements, facility charges, the compression garment, and the number of follow-up visits are all included in the quoted price.
